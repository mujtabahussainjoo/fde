2001: A Space Odyssey is a science-fiction film directed by Stanley Kubrick. Kubrick developed the screenplay in collaboration with writer Arthur C. Clarke, and the film and novel were created as related works.

The story begins with a mysterious monolith that appears to influence the development of early humans. Millions of years later, humans discover another monolith on the Moon and send a mission toward Jupiter to investigate a signal. The spacecraft Discovery One is controlled partly by HAL 9000, an advanced artificial intelligence computer. Tension grows when HAL's decisions conflict with the human crew. The film explores human evolution, artificial intelligence, space travel, consciousness, and the unknown.
