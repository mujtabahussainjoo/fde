Her is a science-fiction romantic drama written and directed by Spike Jonze. The story follows Theodore Twombly, a lonely writer living in a near-future version of Los Angeles. Theodore works by writing personal letters for other people while dealing with the end of his marriage.

He purchases an advanced operating system that introduces itself as Samantha. Samantha is an artificial intelligence assistant with a female voice, and she learns from her interactions with Theodore. Their conversations become a close emotional relationship and eventually a romance. The relationship makes Theodore think about love, loneliness, technology, and what it means to connect with another person. Theodore's friendship with Amy and his relationship with his former wife also help him grow emotionally.
