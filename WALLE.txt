WALL-E is an animated science-fiction film about a small waste-collecting robot left on Earth after humans abandon the planet because of excessive pollution and waste. WALL-E spends years compacting trash and collecting interesting objects. He meets EVE, a more advanced robot sent to search for signs of plant life.

WALL-E follows EVE into space and reaches the Axiom, a large spacecraft where human passengers have lived for generations. The robots discover that a growing plant may show that Earth can support life again. Their mission helps the humans remember Earth and begin the journey home. The film explores environmental responsibility, technology, consumerism, friendship, and hope.
